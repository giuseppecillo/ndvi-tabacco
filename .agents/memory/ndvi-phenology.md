---
name: NDVI phenology reference
description: Decisions for keeping the tobacco NDVI reference, seedling age, and nitrogen interpretation agronomically coherent.
---

**Rule:** Calculate optimal NDVI from a continuous post-transplant phenology curve. Translate seedling age at transplant into a modest number of equivalent phenological days instead of adding a fixed NDVI increment.

**Why:** A fixed NDVI offset produces artificial discontinuities and keeps affecting the result at late stages, while a developmental-age offset is naturally reflected in the curve as the crop matures.

**How to apply:** Keep the biological NDVI reference independent of the nitrogen dose to avoid circular reasoning. Compare total nitrogen with yield-based uptake separately, and use NDVI variance/CV to decide whether the field mean is representative enough for a uniform recommendation.
